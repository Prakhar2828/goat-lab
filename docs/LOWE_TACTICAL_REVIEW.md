# Zach Lowe Comparative and Tactical Review

## Scope

This review extracts structured claims from:

- `LOWE_GOAT_DEBATE_2020`
- `LOWE_LAKERS_DEFENSE_2020`

Both documents belong to the same source family:

```text
lowe_espn_tactical
```

They count as one independent family in consensus even when both
documents support the same player or dimension.

## Evidence roles

The GOAT comparison contributes matched Jordan and LeBron evidence on:

- Late-clock and playoff offense
- Half-court scoring burden
- Turnover management
- Passing execution and advantage creation
- LeBron's rim deterrence and positional flexibility

The Lakers defense article contributes one-season tactical evidence on:

- Help positioning
- Rotation timing
- Passing-lane disruption
- Communication and recognition
- Effort consistency
- Playoff adaptability

## Temporal labels

Jordan claims are labeled `career_through_1998` because the tactical
and late-game discussion centers on his Bulls career.

LeBron comparative claims are labeled `career_through_2020`.

The Lakers defensive claims are labeled `lakers_2019_20`.

None of these claims is labeled `career`.

## Qualification rules

The review preserves several limitations:

- The GOAT article mixes statistical comparison, selected plays,
  historical context, and narrative perception.
- The Lakers article evaluates LeBron inside an elite team defense
  coached by Frank Vogel and anchored by Anthony Davis.
- One title season cannot establish full-career defensive consistency.
- Multiple Lowe articles do not create multiple independent families.
- Selected film examples are not treated as a possession-complete sample.

## Model eligibility

These claims can strengthen corroboration and reveal disagreement, but
they cannot enter the primary expert-film score unless the full
career-coverage and matched-player requirements are later satisfied.
