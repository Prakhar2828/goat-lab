# Thinking Basketball Anchor Review

## Scope

This review extracts structured claims from:

- `TB_MJ_BACKPICKS_2018`
- `TB_LBJ_BACKPICKS_2018`

The two documents belong to the same source family:

```text
thinking_basketball_backpicks
```

They therefore count as one independent family in consensus.

## Temporal coverage

The Jordan profile primarily evaluates his career through the 1998 retirement and does not provide a full Washington-era review.

The LeBron profile was published in April 2018 and does not cover most of his Lakers career.

Claims are therefore labeled:

```text
career_through_1998
career_through_2018
```

They are not labeled `career`.

## Claim procedure

Each claim records:

- Player
- Career phase
- Side
- Dimension
- Direction
- Strength
- Confidence
- Evidence type
- Whether film examples are present
- Whether a sample size is disclosed
- Supporting source location
- Paraphrased summary
- Material limitations
- Review status

No long quotation or copyrighted video is stored.

## Eligibility

These claims can:

- Appear in the evidence explorer
- Establish initial dimension coverage
- Identify corroboration needs
- Contribute to source-disagreement analysis

They cannot enter the primary expert-film score until:

- Career coverage is complete
- At least three independent source families support the dimension
- At least one family is Tier A
- Both players have comparable coverage
- Counterevidence has been reviewed

## Numeric interpretation

Claim direction, strength, and confidence create diagnostic source-family values.

They are not final player grades. No single analyst determines the GOAT Lab offense, defense, or overall result.
